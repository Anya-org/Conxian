# Conxian: Sovereign Infrastructure Monetization (March 2026 Revision)

**Philosophy:** Conxian monetizes the provision of high-integrity, non-custodial infrastructure. Our revenue model is designed to be disruptively low-cost for users while maintaining sustainable B2B and technical service streams.

## 1. Sovereignty-Adjusted Fee (SAF)
To align user behavior with best practices, Conxian uses a dynamic fee model for bridging and swaps.
- **Base Fee**: 0.25%.
- **Discounts**: Up to 50% based on account age and volume.
- **Sovereignty Multiplier**: Up to 20% discount for using StrongBox/TEE and verified backups.
- **Floor Fee (Founder's Cut)**: 0.1%. This is the hardcoded minimum fee required for protocol sustainability (SPEC-REV-001).

## 2. Revenue Distribution (CXIP-013)
All protocol fees above the 0.1% Founder's Cut are distributed via the "Fiscal Dam" system:
- **LP Incentives**: 15%
- **Treasury**: 45%
- **Bounties**: 30%
- **Grants**: 5%
- **Buy-back**: 5%

## 3. B2B & Developer SDK Revenue
- **Conclave SDK Licensing**: Tiered access for fintechs and dApp developers to integrate hardware-enclosed signing.
- **Infrastructure-as-a-Service**: Fees for high-compute actions such as BitVM proof verification and cross-chain message relaying via Nexus.

## 4. Agentic Commerce
- **Mandate Generation**: Small service fees for generating cryptographically signed AP2 mandates within the enclave.
- **Atomic Channel Settlement**: Convenience fees for coordinating A402 atomic service channels.
