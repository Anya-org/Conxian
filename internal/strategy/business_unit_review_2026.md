# Conxian Labs: Business Unit Review (February 2026)

## 1. Conxius Wallet (The Access Layer)
- **Role**: Sovereign Android Vault (B2C Interface).
- **Technical Maturity**: **Production-Ready** (162+ tests, TEE/StrongBox hardened).
- **UVP**: Hardware-grade security on-device without external dependencies. Native Tor/Lightning/L2 support.
- **Monetization**: SAF Model (0.1% - 0.25% fees), Conclave Pro SaaS (.99/mo), Affiliate commissions.
- **Market Positioning**: "The Citadel in your pocket." Targets "digital citadelists" and retail users seeking institutional-grade security on mobile.

## 2. Conxian Gateway (The Routing Layer)
- **Role**: High-Performance Institutional Bridge (B2B Interface).
- **Technical Maturity**: **Institutional-Grade** (Rust/Actix-actor system).
- **UVP**: Orchestrates cross-chain atomic swaps (Stacks, Liquid, Rootstock) with MVCR-compliant reporting.
- **Monetization**: Tiered SDK Licensing (.5k - 5k+/mo), Proof-as-a-Service, Relay-as-a-Service.
- **Market Positioning**: "The B2B Onramp." Targets corporate treasuries, fintechs, and banks needing compliant Bitcoin liquidity.

## 3. Conxian Nexus (The State Layer)
- **Role**: Glass Node & Decentralized Risk Oracle (Middleware).
- **Technical Maturity**: **Robust** (Rust/Tokio/gRPC).
- **UVP**: Verifiable state proofs, FSOC sequencing (MEV mitigation), and real-time risk scoring.
- **Monetization**: Data feeds for institutional risk management, settlement verification services.
- **Market Positioning**: "The Transparency Engine." Targets institutions requiring cryptographic proof of protocol health.

## 4. Conxian Finance (The Settlement Layer)
- **Role**: Stacks-Native DeFi Protocol (Smart Contracts).
- **Technical Maturity**: **Mature Stable** (Clarity 4 / Nakamoto Native).
- **UVP**: Automated revenue distribution (Fiscal Dam V4) and sovereign settlement anchored to Bitcoin burn-height.
- **Monetization**: Protocol fees (SAF integration), liquidity provision incentives.
- **Market Positioning**: "The Decentralized Bank." Targets Bitcoin productive capital and long-term yield seekers.

## 5. Conxian UI (The Discovery Layer)
- **Role**: Unified Web Lens (Web Interface).
- **Technical Maturity**: **Fully Aligned** (Next.js 15 / React 19).
- **UVP**: Single interface for managing swaps, bridging, and yield across all Bitcoin layers.
- **Monetization**: Indirect (drives volume to Gateway/Finance), potential "Featured Protocols" placement.
- **Market Positioning**: "The Front-Office." Targets web-native users and analysts.

## 6. StacksOrbit (The Ops Layer)
- **Role**: Professional Deployment & Monitoring Tool (Developer Tooling).
- **Technical Maturity**: **Advanced** (Python TUI/GUI).
- **UVP**: Automated "Root-Up" deployment with Clarity 4 auto-detection.
- **Monetization**: Free for ecosystem growth, potential premium enterprise monitoring features.
- **Market Positioning**: "The Developer's Workbench." Targets infrastructure providers and ecosystem developers.

## 7. Conxius Platform (The Orchestration Layer)
- **Role**: System Orchestration and Local Dev Stacks.
- **Technical Maturity**: **Fully Aligned** (Docker/Make-based).
- **UVP**: "Glass Node Architecture" observability (Prometheus/Grafana) and standardized dev workflows.
- **Monetization**: Internal efficiency tool, reduces DevOps COGS.
- **Market Positioning**: The "Operating System" of Conxian Labs.
