# Holistic Business Unit Review: Conxian Labs (Phase 5 Transition)

- **Health**: GREEN (Architecture is modular, M1-M11 complete).
- **Market Readiness**: HIGH (Phase 5 "Real Rails" alignment is ahead of incumbents).

## 2. Technical Debt & Risk
- **Risk**: Capacitor-to-Native migration (conxius-wallet) is a critical path bottleneck.

## 3. Critical Gaps

2. **Launch "Conxient" Alpha**: Roll out AI allocation insights to build UBI (Phase 6).

5. **Universal Bitcoin Identity (UBI)**: Attest early users to create a reputation-based moat.
