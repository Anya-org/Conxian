# Standardized Product Brief: Conxian Nexus
- **Role**: Glass Node & Decentralized Risk Oracle (The State Layer)
- **Architecture**: Rust (Tokio, Axum, Tonic), Merkle-based state tracking.
- **UVP**: Bridges off-chain state with Stacks L1, providing verifiable proofs, FSOC sequencing (MEV mitigation), and real-time risk scoring.
- **Dependencies**: PostgreSQL, Redis, gRPC, Hiro API.
- **Positioning**: The "Transparency Engine" for verifiable protocol health.
