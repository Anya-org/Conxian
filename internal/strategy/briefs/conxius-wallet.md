# Standalone Business Unit Brief: Conxius (Access Business)

## 1. Executive Overview
**Conxius** is the high-security access vertical of the Conxian ecosystem. It provides the "Sovereign Android Vault" — a mobile-native interface that commoditizes hardware-grade security (TEE/StrongBox) for the Bitcoin economy.

## 2. Market Analysis (2026 Forecast)
### 2.1 Total Addressable Market (TAM)
- **Market**: Global Mobile Digital Wallets.
- **Size**: $3.6 Trillion (Transaction Value); 5.2 Billion users.
- **Segment**: Self-custody mobile assets representing **$250 Billion** in stored value.

### 2.2 Serviceable Available Market (SAM)
- **Market**: Bitcoin-native mobile users utilizing Layer 2 and Sidechain protocols (Stacks, Liquid, Rootstock, BOB).
- **Size**: **$150 Billion**.

### 2.3 Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM)
- **Target**: Institutional Bitcoin treasuries, high-net-worth "Citadelists," and sovereign retail users requiring "Zero Secret Egress."
- **Size**: **$5 Billion - $10 Billion**.

### 3.3 Revenue Model
- **Sovereignty-Adjusted Fee (SAF)**: 0.1% - 0.25% convenience fee on in-app swaps and bridge transfers.

## 4. Competitive Moat
- **Hardware-Anchored Counter-Positioning**: Unlike MetaMask or Xverse (software-first), Conxius anchors all signatures in the **StrongBox**, reducing the attack surface by 10x and rendering software-only vulnerabilities obsolete.
- **Agentic Integration**: Native **Kotlin MCP Server** allows Conxius to be the signing bridge for AI financial agents, a market where security is the primary barrier to entry.
