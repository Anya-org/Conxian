# Standardized Product Brief: Conxius Wallet
- **Role**: Sovereign Android Vault (The Access Layer)
- **Architecture**: Native Android (Kotlin/Rust) + Android TEE/StrongBox.
- **UVP**: Hardware-grade security (TEE) for the entire Bitcoin ecosystem without external hardware.
- **Dependencies**: BDK (Bitcoin Dev Kit), Android Keystore, Stacks SDK.
- **Positioning**: The "Citadel in your pocket" for retail and institutional signing.
