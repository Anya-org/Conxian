# Conxian: The Sovereign Flywheel (Business Unit Synergies)

While Conxius, CSF, Fusion, and Nexus operate as standalone business units with unique TAMs, they are vertically integrated to create a powerful strategic flywheel for the Sovereign Autonomous Business (SAB).

## 1. The Synergetic Loop

1.  **Nexus State** feeds verified cryptographic truth to **Fusion Gateway**.
2.  **Fusion Gateway** routes institutional liquidity from legacy ERPs into **CSF (Finance)** via ALEX/Portal SDKs.
3.  **CSF** generates automated Bitcoin yield and secures a hardcoded 0.1% Founder's Cut for sustainability.
4.  **Conxius (Access)** acts as the universal hardware-secured signing interface for all three verticals, capturing B2C retail traffic and onboarding it into the B2B ecosystem.

## 2. Shared Moat: The Conclave Stack
All business units utilize the **Conclave Stack** (TEE-native security), ensuring that competitive advantages in one business (e.g., Conxius' hardware isolation) immediately benefit others (e.g., Fusion's attested compliance).

## 3. Operational Efficiency
- **Nexus-First State**: Nexus acts as the centralized indexing layer, reducing redundant cloud costs for Fusion and CSF.
- **Autonomous Growth**: The 5-5-5 referral engine in CSF rewards Conxius users for onboarding others, creating a self-sustaining loop.
