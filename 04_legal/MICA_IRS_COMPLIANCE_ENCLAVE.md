# Institutional Compliance: Hardware-Attested Reporting (2026)

## 1. Executive Summary
Conxian leverages the Android StrongBox/TEE architecture to provide institutional-grade transparency without centralized data custody. We generate **Hardware-Attested Compliance Reports** that satisfy regulatory requirements while maintaining 100% non-custodial sovereignty.

## 2. Mathematically Verifiable Auditing
Unlike traditional self-reported logs, Conxian's auditing primitives are rooted in the Secure Enclave:
- **Immutable Metadata**: Every transaction signed within "The Vault" includes hardware-attested metadata packets.
- **Protocol-Level Validation**: AI agent transactions are verified against human-signed **AP2 Mandates** before execution, ensuring a non-repudiable audit trail of permission.
- **Cryptographic Proofs**: External auditors can verify the integrity of reported transactions using the enclave's public attestation key.

## 3. MiCA & IRS Alignment
- **Secure Custody**: Our non-custodial architecture ensures the user remains the legal operator, aligning with MiCA's secure custody mandates.
- **Reporting Standards**: The system is designed to egress hardware-signed logs compatible with IRS 1099-DA basis tracking.
