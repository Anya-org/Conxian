## **🎯 EXECUTIVE SUMMARY**

Based on comprehensive repository analysis and research, **Conxian Protocol is designed to be classified as a "Sovereign Autonomous Business (SAB)"** - specifically a **Tier 0 Financial Infrastructure Platform** that operates as a **hybrid between a sovereign autonomous bank and a multi-service financial corporation**.

## **📊 BUSINESS CLASSIFICATION ANALYSIS**

### **Primary Classification: Sovereign Autonomous Business (SAB)**

**Why SAB over traditional DAO/Bank/Company:**

1. **Sovereign Authority**: Conxian operates with blockchain-native sovereignty through Bitcoin anchoring, not dependent on traditional legal frameworks
1. **Autonomous Operations**: The `conxian-operations-engine.clar` acts as an "executive branch" with automated governance degradation prevention
1. **Business Services**: Provides actual financial services (DEX, lending, vaults, compliance) - not just governance
1. **Multi-Council Structure**: 5-tier council system (CXD, CXVG, CXTR, CXS, CXLP) mimics corporate board structure but with blockchain sovereignty

### **Secondary Classification Elements**

- **Sovereign Autonomous Bank**: Provides banking services (lending, vaults, payments) with Bitcoin-native settlement
- **Financial Services Corporation**: Multi-service platform offering institutional-grade financial infrastructure
- **Protocol-as-a-Service**: PaaS factory enables deployment of compliant sub-DAOs
