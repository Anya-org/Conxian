# Cross-Chain Agentic Orchestration: The Autonomous Office Manager

## 1. Overview
The **Autonomous Office Manager** (AOM) is the next evolution of the Conxian SAB. It extends the protocol's intelligence layer across multiple blockchain environments, enabling unified position and liquidity management.

## 2. Capabilities
- **Multi-Chain Awareness**: Real-time monitoring of GCR and TVL across Stacks, Bitcoin, and EVM.
- **Autonomous Maneuvers**: Execution of cross-chain swaps and yield rebalancing using LayerZero V2 and Axelar.
- **Operational Heartbeat**: Coordination of multi-agent tasks (Risk, Treasury, Compliance) via a synchronized "Dual-Clock" heartbeat.

## 3. Trust and Safety
- **Sovereign Guard**: All cross-chain instructions must be signed by the AOM and verified by the destination chain's `conxian-access` module.
- **Fail-Closed Routing**: If interoperability providers (LayerZero/Axelar) exhibit abnormal latency or state drift, the AOM autonomously closes cross-chain bridges and retreats to the Stacks "Safe Haven".

## 4. Future Roadmap (Phase 4)
- Integration of **ZKML** (Zero-Knowledge Machine Learning) for predictive agentic logic that is verifiable on-chain.
- Deployment of the AOM as a decentralized autonomous service (DAS) on the Nexus Network.
