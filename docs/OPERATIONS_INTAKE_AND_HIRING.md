# Conxian Labs: Operations, Hiring Mandates, and Triggers

This document outlines the strategic decisions regarding hiring, operations, and the split of economic rights for Conxian Labs and the Conxian Protocol.

## 1. Hiring Mandates (Wave 1)
To reach protocol maturity, the following roles are defined with clear mandates:

### Engineering Lead
- **Mandate**: Own the Clarity implementation and Gateway scaling.
- **Success Criteria**: 0 production-path contamination; 100% test coverage for core SAB modules.

### Security & Infrastructure Lead
- **Mandate**: Manage the ZSE policy and Sovereign Node distribution.
- **Success Criteria**: Successful Akash/Kwil deployment patterns; Zero breach incidents.

### Product & Execution Lead
- **Mandate**: Bridge protocol capabilities to commercial XaaP pilots.
- **Success Criteria**: Delivery of 3 successful institutional pilots; Validated pricing model.

## 2. Hiring Triggers (Runway-Based)
No paid hiring is activated until the following **Minimum Paid Hiring Trigger** is met:
1. **Payout path is real and verifiable**: ConxianCSF mainnet launch is complete.
2. **Milestone Verification**: Successful delivery of Phase 1 (Mainnet Genesis).
3. **Funding Depth**: 6 months of runway secured for the proposed role.

## 3. Economic Rights Reconciliation
The split of economic rights is explicitly mapped as follows:
- **Public Protocol**: Owns the core CSF logic, BME engine, and public liquidity fees.
- **Conxian Labs**: Owns the Gateway proprietary logic, managed hosting revenue (Lane B), and enterprise license fees (Lane C).
- **Contributors**: Entitled to merit-based emissions from the BME engine and specific bounty rewards.
- **Founders**: Bound by long-term vesting and performance-gated claims in the Operational Treasury.

## 4. Intake Flow
All external interest (Partners, Contributor inquiries) must follow the **Partner Intake Flow**:
1. **Initial Assessment**: Alignment with Sovereign Ethos.
2. **Capability Check**: Technical/Business fit for XaaP Lanes.
3. **ExCo Approval**: Review in weekly Linear-first ExCo sessions.
