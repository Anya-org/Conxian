## 2. Governance-Minimized Scaling

The Conxian ecosystem scales through **Policy-as-Code**, ensuring that "independent" does not mean "opaque" or "insecure."

### Permissionless Onboarding
Any laboratory or developer can build on the Conxian CNS Core without explicit permission from Botshelo or a committee. The protocol itself enforces compliance through the **BOS Kernel**.

### Sovereign Guardrails
The platform's **Guardian Class** wallets enforce a cryptographic floor (the "StrongBox").
- **Time-Locks**: Payouts and high-risk state changes are subject to a 144-block Bitcoin-anchored Time-Lock.
- **State-Proofs**: All logic execution must be accompanied by a multi-sig state-proof verified by the Nexus network.

### Auditable Logic (ZSE Compliance)
Every independent lab must publish their logic to a public, Zero Secret Egress (ZSE) compliant repository (modeled after `conxian-business`). This ensures that the platform remains auditable while protecting sensitive operational secrets in the Linear Virtual Office.
