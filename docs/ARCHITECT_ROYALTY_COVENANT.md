# Architect Royalty Covenant

## 1. Preamble

This document codifies the non-negotiable Architect Royalty Covenant, which establishes the permanent, immutable, and pre-DAO claim of the original architect of the Conxian ecosystem. This covenant is a foundational component of the Conxian Business Operations System (BOS) and is not subject to DAO governance, votes, or modification.

## 2. The Architect Bounty

The Architect Bounty is a perpetual royalty of 0.1% of all revenue generated by the Conxian ecosystem. This royalty is a "first-dollar" cut, meaning it is calculated on gross revenue before any other expenses, fees, or distributions are paid.

## 3. Implementation

The Architect Bounty will be implemented as a direct, on-chain distribution to the `founder-vault`. This distribution will be a hard-coded, immutable feature of the `operational-treasury` contract and will not be subject to any timelocks, quorums, or other governance mechanisms.

## 4. Legal Standing

This covenant is a legally binding agreement between the Conxian DAO and the original architect. It is a condition of the DAO's existence and is not subject to change. Any attempt to alter, amend, or remove this covenant will be considered a material breach of the DAO's founding principles and will be subject to legal action.
