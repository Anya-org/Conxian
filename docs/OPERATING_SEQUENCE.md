# Conxian Operating Sequence & Proof Gates

## 1. Operating Order
As of April 2026, the Conxian Sovereign Autonomous Business (SAB) follows a strictly prioritized operating sequence to ensure systemic stability and Bitcoin-anchored integrity.

1.  **ConxianCSF (Common Settlement Framework)**: The foundational liquidity and settlement layer. Must be fully operational and verified before other layers.
2.  **Creator/Community Economy**: Builders, agents, and users. This layer leverages CSF for value creation and distribution.
3.  **Conxian Gateway**: The institutional bridge (ISO 20022/ERP). Activated last to bring external capital into the verified on-chain economy.

## 2. Core Rule: Creator Freedom
Treat creator and developer freedom as a core system rule. The economy is designed to:
- Encourage builders, users, and agents.
- Maintain payout discipline through automated proof-of-work/merit markers.
- Ensure launch claims are strictly verified.

## 3. Proof Gates
Each transition in the operating sequence is gated by minimum proof requirements:
- **CSF Gate**: 100% contract compliance, ALEX adapter verification, and BME engine initialization.
- **Economy Gate**: Community funding model activation and bounty workflow enforcement.
- **Gateway Gate**: ISO 20022 parser validation and SAP/Oracle OData V4 normalization verification.
