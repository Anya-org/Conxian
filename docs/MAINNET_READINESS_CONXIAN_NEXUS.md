Detailed launch readiness evidence, signer controls, environment bindings, and operational attestations are maintained in private governance and operations records.

### 3) Institutional ingress and control surface
- External integration readiness is tracked through private release gates.
- Sensitive attestation and runtime enforcement details are intentionally excluded from public docs.

### 4) Release hygiene

- Public documents provide high-level status only; operational proof artifacts remain private.
