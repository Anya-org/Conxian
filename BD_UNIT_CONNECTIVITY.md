**Market**: Enterprise ERP & Institutional Treasury.
**Brand**: Fusion Gateway.
**Positioning**: The deterministic ISO 20022 bridge between legacy financial systems and sovereign Bitcoin rails.

## 3. Targeted Segments
- **Fortune 500 CFOs**: Seeking to add Bitcoin to the balance sheet with audit-ready reconciliation.

- **Supply Chain Networks**: Utilizing "Payment-on-Receipt" (PoR) escrow flows.
