# The Conxian Protocol: A Guarded Experiment.

## The Pledge
We prioritize safety over speed. We cap risk so we can scale trust.

## The Velvet Rope
To ensure a stable and secure launch, access to the Conxian Protocol is initially limited. The first 1,000 users can claim a "Genesis Badge," securing their spot in the ecosystem. This scarcity ensures quality and allows us to grow the protocol responsibly.

## The Viral Key
Growth is essential, but it must be controlled. Once the Genesis spots are filled, new users can only join via an invite from an existing member. Want to invite your friends? Prove you are real (KYC). Verification is the only way to grow the network, turning compliance into a valuable asset.

## The Exit
We believe in liquidity. In the world of DeFi, your assets should never be trapped. If the underlying chain experiences congestion and sBTC withdrawals are slow, you can instantly receive an "Exit Ticket" NFT. This ticket represents your pending withdrawal and can be traded on any secondary market. You are never trapped in Conxian.
