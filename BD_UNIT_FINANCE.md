# BD Strategy: Conxian Sovereign Finance (CSF) (Finance Business)

**Market**: Bitcoin-Native DeFi & Institutional Liquidity.
**Brand**: Conxian CSF.
**Positioning**: The high-integrity settlement layer for the productive Bitcoin economy.

## 1. Revenue Streams
- **Protocol Fees**: Hardcoded 0.1% "Founder's Cut" on all transactional events (Swaps, Lending).
- **Performance Fees**: 10% of generated yield from automated liquidity pools and vaults.
- **Mint/Burn Fees**: 0.05% for asset wrapping (e.g., ART, USDCx).

## 2. Competitive Advantage: "Gift Status"
- **Autonomous Sovereignty**: Transition to ExecutorDAO ensures the protocol is a public utility, attracting institutional volume wary of centralized "backdoors."
- **Nakamoto-Ready**: Native integration with Stacks sBTC and ALEX/Portal SDKs.

## 3. Targeted Segments
- **DeFi Protocols**: Seeking standardized liquidity integration via the Conxian Standard Format (CSF).
- **Institutional LPs**: Looking for transparent, non-custodial yield on idle Bitcoin capital.
- **Ecosystem Builders**: Utilizing Conxian as the "backend" for new financial products.
