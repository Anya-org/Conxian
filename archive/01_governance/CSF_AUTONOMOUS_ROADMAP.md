# CSF: Autonomous Sovereignty Roadmap (Phase 6)

This document defines the strategic transition of Conxian Sovereign Finance (CSF) to a fully autonomous, "Gift Status" entity.

## 1. Governance Transition (ExecutorDAO)
- **Phase A (Months 1-12)**: Single-admin control for rapid parameter tuning.
- **Phase B (Months 13-24)**: Multi-sig Institutional Quorum (n-of-m) via Musig2.
- **Phase C (Month 25+)**: ExecutorDAO handover. All critical parameters governed by CXD token holders.

## 2. Key Relinquishment (Gift Status)
The final act of the core development team is the strategic relinquishment of administrative authority.
- **Mechanism**: The `relinquish-admin` function in the `autonomous-registry` contract.
- **Effect**: Sets the admin principal to `SP000000000000000000002Q6VF78` (the burn address).
- **Result**: The protocol becomes an immutable "Gift" to the Bitcoin ecosystem, governed solely by its hardcoded logic and DAO mandates.

## 3. Revenue Loops & Sustainability
- **Founder's Cut**: A permanent 0.1% fee on all protocol transactions to support long-term maintenance.
- **CXIP-013**: Autonomous distribution of protocol revenue across 6 dedicated vaults (LP, Treasury, Bounty, Grants, Buyback, Insurance).
- **5-5-5 Engine**: Autonomous referral-driven growth distributing fees to referrers, referees, and protocol health.
