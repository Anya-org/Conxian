# Vision & Mission (2026 Revision)

## Vision
To establish a future where human discretion is replaced by mathematical certainty, anchored in the sovereignty of Bitcoin and the absolute authority of code within a **Sovereign Autonomous Business (SAB)**.

## Mission
Conxian builds the high-integrity infrastructure required for community-owned, autonomous protocols. We enable trustless, non-custodial financial operations by tying system states to the Bitcoin burn-block height and enforcing "Code is Law."

## Core Principle
**Code is Law. Sovereignty is Absolute.**
