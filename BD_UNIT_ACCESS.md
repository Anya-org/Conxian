**Market**: Mobile Self-Custody & SME Treasury.
**Brand**: Conxius Wallet.
**Positioning**: The world's most secure and user-friendly portal for agentic Bitcoin finance.

## 1. Revenue Streams
- **Convenience Fees**: 0.1% - 0.25% on in-wallet swaps and bridge executions.
- **Pro Subscriptions**: $9.99/mo for advanced AI insights, multi-sig quorums, and "Citadel" node support.

- **Shared Conclaves**: Viral multi-sig onboarding where existing users "vouch" for new entrants via hardware-anchored attestations.
- **Agentic Onboarding**: AI-led wallet setup that abstracts private key complexity while maintaining hardware-enclosed security.

## 3. Targeted Segments
- **High-Net-Worth Individuals**: Requiring "Citadel" grade security on mobile.
- **SME Treasuries**: Moving from centralized exchanges to self-custody with internal quorums.
- **Agentic Economy**: AI agents requiring hardware-enclosed mandates for autonomous spending.
